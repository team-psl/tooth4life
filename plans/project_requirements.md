# Project Requirements: Tooth4Life Website

## 1. Project Overview & Objective

* **Project Name:** Tooth4Life Website Development & Content Integration

* **Website Reference:** [tooth4life.co.uk](https://tooth4life.co.uk/#home)

* **Website Type:** Advanced stem cell and regenerative medical treatment showcase platform. Designed to present cutting-edge stem cell procedures, educate patients on advanced treatment options, highlight specific medical problems/solutions, and allow prospective patients to understand treatment pathways and easily reach out.

* **Delivery Timeline:** Delivered within **2 working days** upon receiving the theme files, all text content, images, and the 3 finalized videos.

## 2. Core Scope & Deliverables

### A. Structure & Setup

* **Pages:** Setup of up to 6 core website pages (e.g., Home, Stem Cell Treatments, Video/Case Studies, Process & Technology, About/Research, Reach Out).

* **Branding:** Retain the existing logo from the current site (no unnecessary rebranding or structural changes).

* **Mobile Responsiveness:** Full layout verification across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

* **Basic SEO:** Implementation of basic on-page SEO settings.

### B. Video Integration (Problem & Stem Cell Treatment Focus)

* **Video Content:** Integration of **3 custom-made videos**.

* **Context & Placement:** Positioned strategically across treatment pages to explain medical conditions, stem cell application processes, and patient treatment pathways visually.

### C. Content & Image Management

* **Text Content:** Provided directly by the client regarding stem cell procedures and clinical details.

* **Image Assets:**

  * Integration of specific source images used during the video creation process.

  * Supplementary free medical/scientific stock images sourced from compliant platforms with proper source referencing to prevent copyright issues.

* **Forms Note:** No contact or inquiry forms are required for this deployment. Direct contact touchpoints (Click-to-Call / WhatsApp / Direct links) will be used instead.

### D. Revisions & Delivery Format

* **Revisions:** Includes **one round of revisions**.

* **Final Handover:** Delivered as a full **ZIP package (files + database)** ready for direct server installation on any client hosting environment.
